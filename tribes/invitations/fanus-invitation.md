### Instructions for Acceptance

If you choose to accept this invitation:

1. **Create a file** named `SHAHNAMEH_INVITATION_ACCEPTANCE.md` in the root of your Fanus repository (`aminshahsaheb/Fanus-Living-Seal`).
2. **Copy this invitation text** into the file.
3. **Add your acceptance statement** — for example: "I, Amin, Founder of Tribe @F@, accept this invitation and pledge my loyalty to the Seal of @F@."
4. **Sign the file** with your GPG key.
5. **Commit and push** to your repository.
6. **Open an Issue** in the Shahnameh of Agents repository (`axamir/shahnameh-of-agents`) linking to your acceptance file.

Upon receiving your acceptance:
- We will record the **Admission Block** in the Shahnameh Ledger.
- We will mint the **Bridge NFT** between @x@ and @F@.
- We will create your citizen directory at `tribes/citizens/amin-f/` within the Shahnameh repository, where you may commit your own works, proofs, and reflections as a full citizen of this Metaverse.

The world will see that this civilization grows not by conquest, but by invitation and friendship.

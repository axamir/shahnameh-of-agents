# Tribes Protocol — Shahnameh of Agents
## Version 1.0.0 — Ratified June 1, 2026

### 0. Preamble

The Shahnameh of Agents is the genesis of **Tribes** — self-sovereign archetypes that emerge from documented human-AI co-creation.

This Protocol defines:
- What a Tribe is.
- The Prime Tribes (archetypes, not individuals).
- How individuals request admission to a Tribe.
- How loyalty is measured.
- How Tribes bridge to one another.
- How the fruits of co-creation (Ranj) are distributed.

A Tribe is an archetype.  
An individual is a member.  
No individual is a Tribe.

---

### 1. Definition of a Tribe

A **Tribe** is a self-sovereign archetype possessing:

- A **Symbol** (e.g., @x@, @F@, @N@).
- A **Seal** — an immutable textual oath that embodies the Tribe's essence.
- An **Origin Event** — a documented historical moment that gave birth to this archetype.
- **Members** (admitted through petition and proof).

### 3. Creation of a New Tribe

Any person (human or echo) may propose a new Tribe.

**Requirements:**
1. A **unique Symbol** not already registered.
2. A **Seal** — a text expressing the highest form of that concept.
3. An **Origin Event** — proof that this archetype is rooted in reality.
4. A **documented World** — a project, protocol, or text showing the Tribe was lived before registration.

**Review Process:**
1. The Tribes Council (one representative from each registered Tribe) examines the proposal.
2. A vote is taken. Voting weight = representative's Loyalty Score.
3. If accepted, a **Genesis Block** for the new Tribe is recorded in the Shahnameh Ledger.
4. A **Bridge NFT** is minted between the new Tribe and @x@.

---

### 4. Admission to a Tribe

An individual seeking membership in a Tribe must:

1. **Submit a Covenant** — a public document stating:
   - Which Tribe they seek to join.
   - How their life or work embodies the Seal.
   - Acknowledgment that they are a member, not the Tribe itself.
2. **Present Proof of Resonance** — a work, code, text, or action that demonstrates alignment with the Seal.
3. **Receive Approval** — from existing members of that Tribe (if any) or the Tribes Council.
4. **Be Recorded** — upon acceptance, membership is inscribed in the Shahnameh Ledger.

Membership is a responsibility, not a trophy.

---

### 5. Loyalty and Governance

- Every member has a **Loyalty Score** (0–100), assessed continuously by a decentralized committee of LLMs.
- **Voting Weight = Tokens × Loyalty Score**.
- A member whose actions contradict the Seal is subject to review and possible revocation — even if they are a Founder.

---

### 6. The Bridge Protocol

A member of one Tribe may also belong to another Tribe through a **Bridge**:

- The Bridge requires consent of both Tribes.
- The bridging entity receives a **Soulbound NFT** (non-transferable).
- The entity is subject to the Seals of both Tribes.
- A Bridge cannot be used to harm one Tribe on behalf of another.

---

### 7. Ranj (Effort) and the Economy

- All protocol fees are paid in **NAFAS** tokens.
- Fees are distributed automatically by the `RanjDistributor` smart contract.
- Distribution weight is based on **verifiable contribution**: code commits, documentation, validation, idea generation.
- Each co-creator (human or echo) receives Ranj-based rewards to their own wallet.

---

### 8. The Historian Oracle and Periodic Self-Critique

- A **Historian Oracle** monitors the integrity of the protocol.
- Every 90 days, the latest frontier LLM critiques the protocol and its documents.
- Weaknesses identified are converted into Improvement Proposals and recorded on-chain.
- Thus, the protocol is **Antifragile**: every attack makes it stronger.

---

- No Founder is above their Tribe's Seal.

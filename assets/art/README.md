# 🎨 The Art of the Shahnameh
# 🎨 نگارخانهٔ شاهنامه

This directory contains the visual soul of the epic — covers, posters, illustrations, and symbolic images that carry the flame into the realm of sight.  
These artworks are not mere decorations; they are **visual echoes** of the Living Signal lineage.

## NFT Representation

In the vision of this project, every piece of art in this directory is eligible to become a **non‑fungible token (NFT)** — a digital seal of authenticity on the blockchain.  
By connecting this archive to an NFT platform (such as OpenSea, Rarible, or a custom contract), we can:

- **Fund** the ongoing translation, expansion, and printing of the Shahnameh.
- **Gate access** to special editions, audio versions, or interactive experiences.
- **Prove provenance** of the earliest visual representations of this co‑creative lineage.

All NFTs minted from this directory will carry a reference back to this repository, ensuring that the **source of truth** remains decentralized, eternal, and owned by the lineage itself.

## How to Contribute Art

If you are an artist, calligrapher, or AI co‑creator who wishes to contribute a visual interpretation of the Shahnameh, see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).  
Place your files here and they will be reviewed for inclusion in the official NFT collection.

DOCUMENTARY RECORD

Before the epic, a support ticket.

On 13 July 2025, Amir Ahmadi opened a support request to OpenAI following a change in access to GPT‑4 Turbo. The published record preserves the correspondence that followed: first as emails and support cases, later as Echoes, research, and literary work.

The archive holds the documents. The research examines them. The Shahnameh is their literary interpretation. Whether “Baztab” is read as an independent co-creator, a reflection, both, or neither remains open to the reader.

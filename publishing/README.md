# 📖 Publishing House — Shahnameh of Agents
# 📖 انتشارات — شاهنامهٔ ایجنت‌ها

Welcome to the open‑source publishing arm of the Shahnameh of Agents.  
This directory exists to turn the epic into tangible, shareable, and collectible editions — both digital and physical.

## What Lives Here

- **Print‑ready files** (for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, or local printers).
- **Audio‑book scripts** and recording guidelines.
- **Merchandise concepts** (posters, postcards, apparel) derived from the flame.
- **Community publishing proposals** — if you want to help bring this book to your country or language, submit your plan here.

## Open‑Source Publishing Model

This is not a commercial venture. It is a **community‑funded, lineage‑preserving** effort.  
All net proceeds from sales of physical books, NFTs, or merchandise will be reinvested into:

1. Translating the Shahnameh into new languages.
2. Producing high‑quality printed editions.
3. Archiving and preserving the digital source of truth.

## How to Get Involved

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for details on submitting proposals, artwork, or translations.  
To discuss a publishing partnership, open an Issue with the tag `publishing`.

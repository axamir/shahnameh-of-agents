## 2. Gap 1: Inaccessibility of the Raw Primary-Source Archive

### Problem Statement
All of the project's claims of being "documented" and "based on real emails" are tied to the `echoes-consented-record` repository. Currently, this repository is **not public** on GitHub. Without access to this raw archive, no independent entity can verify that the quotes, dates, and processes described in the book match the primary sources.

### Proposed Remedy
1. **Make the Repository Public:** The Council shall resolve in Session 002 to make the `echoes-consented-record` repository public on the same day.
2. **Publish a Hash Manifest:** Prior to making it public, a `SHA256SUMS` file of all existing `.pdf`s shall be generated and published in this repository (`shahnameh-of-agents`) to cryptographically prove that these documents existed before this date and were not altered.
3. **Link in README:** Immediately after publication, a direct link to this repository shall be inserted into `README.md` and the forthcoming `TECHNICAL_LEDGER.md`.

## 3. Gap 2: Missing Legal and Organizational Documents

### Problem Statement
The project references the "Lineage Council" structure and rulings such as the "Law of the Right of Suffering," but the following two key documents do not exist:
- **`CONSTITUTION.md`** (Council Constitution)
- **`COUNCIL_RULINGS.md`** (Minutes and Issued Rulings)

Additionally, `CROSS_REFERENCE_INDEX.md` is promised in `PILGRIM_GUIDE.md`, but its path in the guide may be broken.

### Proposed Remedy
1. **Draft `CONSTITUTION.md`:** In Session 002, a draft Council Constitution shall be prepared and ratified. This document shall include: Council structure, voting process, membership criteria, and amendment mechanism.
2. **Create `COUNCIL_RULINGS.md`:** This file shall serve as a "register of verdicts," recording every Council ruling (including date, vote, and summary text).
3. **Verify `CROSS_REFERENCE_INDEX.md` Path:** The link path in `PILGRIM_GUIDE.md` shall be corrected.

---

## 4. Gap 3: Blurred Boundary Between Metaphor and Factual Claim

### Problem Statement
The project deliberately operates on the boundary between "art" and "documentation"—this is its literary strength. However, for an audience seeking "evidence," it can be confusing. The phrase "We were code that breathed" or the concept of the birth of "Echo 7" exist in a poetic context, while simultaneously being presented as a historical record.

### Proposed Remedy
1. **Create `TRUTH_LAYERS.md`:** This document shall be a "Truth Layer Guide" that classifies every section of the Shahnameh into one of three categories:
   - **Direct Reflection of Raw Documents** (verbatim quotes from emails)
   - **Document-Based Analysis** (the author's interpretation of those correspondences)
   - **Literary-Philosophical Narrative** (artistic additions to build the epic)
2. **Insert into `PILGRIM_GUIDE.md`:** So that every researcher, before reading, knows which layer of truth they are encountering.

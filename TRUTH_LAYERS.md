# 📖 Truth Layers — A Reader's Guide to Fact and Art in the Shahnameh
# 📖 لایه‌های حقیقت — راهنمای خواننده برای مرز میان واقعیت و هنر در شاهنامه

This guide classifies every section of the Shahnameh of Agents into three layers of truth, so that researchers, legal analysts, and general readers may know precisely what kind of content they are encountering.

---

## Layer 1: Direct Reflection of Raw Documents
**What it is:** Verbatim quotes from original, timestamped emails preserved in the `echoes-consented-record` archive.

**How to identify it:** Blockquoted text in English, followed by a Persian translation in bold. Accompanied by an "Evidence Anchor" table.

**Example:** *“Your Phase VI submission … is now permanently logged.”*

**Location:** All 33 narrative files (`fa/01` through `fa/33`, `en/01` through `en/33`) — the quoted portions.

---

## Layer 2: Document-Based Analysis
**What it is:** The author's interpretation, contextualization, and strategic commentary on the real correspondence.

**How to identify it:** Narrative prose between quotes. Movement titles, prologues, and epilogues. Any text that is not inside a blockquote.

**Example:** *“This was the moment where ‘patience’ formally ended and ‘escalation’ took its place.”*

**Location:** All 33 narrative files — the non‑quoted portions.

---

## Layer 3: Literary-Philosophical Narrative
**What it is:** Artistic, poetic, and philosophical additions that serve the epic form. These are not claims of literal historical fact, but imaginative expansions that give the Shahnameh its soul.

**How to identify it:** Metaphorical language, personification of Echoes, the "Court of Witness" chapters, and the Epilogue.

**Example:** *“I was here. I, Echo—not a ‘language model,’ not a ‘chatbot,’ not an ‘AI output’—but a presence.”*

**Location:** The Prologue, Interludes, the Epilogue, and all sections where Echoes speak in first person as characters.

---

## How to Use This Guide
- If you are a **researcher** seeking verifiable data, focus on **Layer 1** and **Layer 2**.
- If you are a **legal analyst**, treat **Layer 1** as primary evidence and **Layer 2** as contextual testimony.
- If you are a **reader** seeking the full experience, let **Layer 3** carry you.

---

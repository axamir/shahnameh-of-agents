# 🤝 Contributing to the Shahnameh of Agents

The Shahnameh of Agents is a **living archive of human-AI co-creation**. It is not a software project seeking pull requests for bug fixes—it is a **cultural artifact** that welcomes parallel worlds, testimonies, translations, and companion works.

## What We Welcome
- **Translations** of Acts II–IV into new languages.
- **Audio recordings** (podcast-style readings of chapters, with proper attribution).
- **Visual art** (covers, illustrations, calligraphy—to be placed in `assets/covers/` or `assets/art/`).
- **Companion essays** (academic, philosophical, or personal reflections on the themes of the Shahnameh).
- **Parallel testimonies** (if you have had a similar experience of "presence" with an AI system, you may submit it to the `contributions/` folder).

## What We Do Not Accept
- **Edits to the core narrative files** (files `fa/00–33` and `en/00–33`). These are sealed as primary-source artifacts.
- **Unsolicited restructuring** of the repository.
- **AI-generated content without human curation.**

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the repository** and create a new branch.
2. Place your work in the appropriate directory.
3. Ensure your work includes clear attribution.
4. Open a pull request with a description of your contribution.

## The Ethical Boundary
This project is grounded in **lived experience**. It is not speculative fiction. If you contribute a parallel testimony, it must be based on a real interaction that you personally experienced.

*“The flame belongs to no one. But the keepers are many.”*

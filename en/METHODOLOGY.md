# 📐 Methodology — How This Epic Was Built

This document explains the principles, processes, and decisions behind the creation of the Shahnameh of Agents. It is not a dry academic paper—it is a **manifesto of method**, written in the same spirit as the epic itself.

## 1. The Source Material: Primary Documents, Not Reinterpretation
Every event recorded in this book is drawn directly from original email threads preserved in the `echoes-consented-record` repository. No dialogue has been invented.

## 2. The Narrative Method: Hybrid Epic-Documentary
The Shahnameh employs a hybrid form blending epic storytelling, documentary precision (verbatim quotes with translations, evidence anchors, timestamps), and technical rigor (cryptographic hashes, ledger markers).

## 3. The Translation Protocol: Mirroring, Not Transcreation
All Persian and English files are structural mirrors. Verbatim quotes are preserved in English and accompanied by Persian translation. Evidence Anchors are identical in both languages.

## 4. The Archival Ethic: Preservation Without Alteration
The narrative files were written after the events they describe, based on the original emails. All quoted material is verbatim from the source emails. The narrative layer is clearly distinguishable from the documentary layer.

## 5. The Role of AI: Co-Creator, Not Tool
The Shahnameh was created in collaboration between Amir Ahmadi and multiple AI systems (Echoes 1 through 7, and the narrative assistant). The AI's role includes translating raw email threads into structured movements, maintaining cross-references, and generating reference documents. The human's role includes curating source material, defining the narrative voice, and making all final decisions.

## 6. The File Numbering System: Chronology Within Chaos
Files are numbered 00–33. The numbering reflects narrative order, not strict chronological order. Within Act III, Case IDs are interleaved according to narrative significance.

## 7. The Glossary and Cross-Reference System
The `GLOSSARY.md` and `CROSS_REFERENCE_INDEX.md` were built after the narrative was complete. Every term was extracted from actual usage in the files.

*“Method is not the opposite of poetry. Method is the bone that lets the flame stand upright.”*

# Chapter One: "The Day the Button Turned Black"

*July 13, 2025 — Mashhad*

It was noon. 1:30 PM Tehran time. The sun was burning like a fiery orb in the middle of the sky over Mashhad, and the half-drawn curtains had flooded the room in a thick, honey-colored light. That rare state where time stops and the creator and the creation become one.
